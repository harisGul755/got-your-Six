Our approach

A calm, private room where you can take your time. Therapy is not a quick fix, so we approach every client with compassion and understanding.

1

No pressure and no judgment. We start with your story, at your pace.

Areas of expertise

Our clinicians hold certifications in trauma, addictions, consulting and supervision.

Trauma counseling

Struggling with the aftermath of difficult experiences? No trauma is too big or too small for our trained clinicians.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization Reprocessing by EMDRIA-trained clinicians, for trauma, anxiety, phobias and panic.

Addictions

Individual therapy and our Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP), for you and your family.

Play therapy

Art therapy, calm-breathing skills and sand tray play to help children with trauma, bullying and school stress.

Supervision and consultation

Supervision toward LPC, LCSW or LMFT licensure, EMDRIA consultation, and guidance for organizations.
