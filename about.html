About us

Veteran-owned, service-minded

Got Your Six Counseling Services is a veteran-owned private practice in Northern Virginia. Our clinicians have served on active duty, in the Reserves, with law enforcement, and as military spouses.

Therapy is a process that takes time, trust and knowledge. We offer a caring, comforting approach built around your needs.

Specialties

  • Family and couples therapy
  • Play therapy and EMDR
  • LGBTQIAPK matters
  • Telemental health
  • Veterans' concerns, trauma and bullying
