Services

VASAP

The Virginia Alcohol Safety Action Program supports people referred after an alcohol- or drug-related driving offense. Our clinicians provide the required education and assessment in a respectful, non-judgmental setting, helping you meet court and program requirements while building healthier habits that protect you, your family, and your community.

Psychodiagnostic assessments

A psychodiagnostic assessment is a structured evaluation that looks at your symptoms, history, and daily functioning to clarify what you are experiencing. Through interviews and standardized measures, we identify a diagnosis when appropriate and recommend a clear treatment plan, giving you and your care team a reliable starting point.

Neuropsychological assessments

Neuropsychological assessments examine how the brain supports memory, attention, problem-solving, language, and emotion. Using standardized testing and a detailed history, we help explain changes in thinking or behavior, whether linked to injury, development, or medical conditions, and provide practical recommendations for school, work, and everyday life.

Biopsychosexual assessments

A biopsychosexual assessment takes a whole-person view of your biological, psychological, and sexual health, along with relationships and life circumstances. In a confidential, respectful conversation, we gather the full picture and use it to guide treatment decisions, referrals, and supportive next steps tailored to your needs.
